LocalMind AI

Your offline study companion.

A private AI chat assistant for students. After a one-time setup it runs on your phone, with no account and no internet connection needed.

Download demo (Android) See features

What it does

Built for explaining, summarizing and practicing, wherever you study.

Works offline

The AI model runs locally on your device. Your questions are not sent to a server.

Private by design

No sign-up, no account, no tracking. Chats stay on your phone.

Chat history

Open the side menu to continue earlier chats, start a new one or delete old ones.

Copy and edit

Press and hold a message to copy it, or edit your own message and ask again.

Voice input

If speech recognition is available on the device, dictate your question with the microphone button.

Light and dark mode

Clean indigo design with smooth animations, a thinking indicator and word-by-word answers.

Your language

The interface follows the phone language (English, Turkish, Turkmen) and the assistant answers in the language you write.

Resumable setup

The model download can resume after an interruption and checks free storage first.

How it works

Three steps, then you are offline.

Install the appOpen LocalMind AI on your Android phone.
One-time model downloadOn first launch the app downloads its language model (about 2.6 GB). Wi-Fi and 3 GB of free space recommended.
Chat anywhereOnce downloaded, ask questions with no internet connection.
